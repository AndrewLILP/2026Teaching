Stage 5 · EIS: Analysing data

Solar Data · demo double lesson

68 years for the first terawatt. Two for the second.

Students survey their class, read twenty-five years of world solar data, decide whether growth is linear or exponential, and test predictions and charts. Lesson 2 moves to a spreadsheet: verify, calculate, predict and build a dashboard.

1954 → 2022: first terawatt 2022 → 2024: second terawatt 2024 → 2026: third terawatt Each terawatt arrived faster than the last. That shrinking gap is what exponential growth looks like.

How fast is solar growing?

Build a solar dashboard

Compare sources, verify a starter file with a planted unit error, write growth formulas, predict with FORECAST, decide with IF, then build and peer-test a one-screen dashboard.
