Keep this handy · the visualisation checklist

  1. Honest headline: the title says what the chart shows without exaggerating.
  2. Labels and source: axes, units and where the data came from are shown.
  3. Honest scale: bars start at zero; any log scale is clearly labelled.
  4. Right chart type: lines for change over time, bars for comparing amounts.
  5. Nothing cherry-picked: the whole relevant time period is shown.
  6. Not colour alone: labels or a legend explain what each colour means.
  7. Sample size or coverage shown: readers can tell how much data, and whose, is in the chart.

Scale check

1 terawatt (TW) = 1,000 gigawatts (GW) = 1,000,000 megawatts (MW). One gigawatt is roughly the rooftop solar on 150,000 Australian homes.
