📋 Coin Collecting Game — quick reference

You don't need to memorise these details — refer back here any time. The questions are testing computing theory, not your memory of the game.

Setting & movement
3D, Mario 64‑inspired world. Player moves with arrow keys or WASD.
Collecting coins
Walking into a coin collects it. Coins do not respawn once collected.
Level 1 coin spawning
Coins spawn at fixed positions.
Level 2 coin spawning
Coins spawn randomly, many at once.
The star
Appears once 20 coins are collected. Collecting the star plays a cutscene and loads a new scene.
Health bar
Counts down like a timer while the player plays. If health drops below 20%, the level resets.
On-screen UI
Displays the current level and the number of coins collected.
