Push Your Luck

Roll again and again to build up your turn total — but roll a 1 and you lose everything you've earned this round. Bank your points before that happens, if you dare.

  • Each round: roll the die as many times as you like.
  • Every roll of 2–6 adds that number to your turn total.
  • Roll a 1 and your turn total is wiped to zero — the round ends.
  • Bank at any time to lock in your turn total and add it to your overall score.
  • Play 5 rounds. Highest overall score wins.

The maths behind it

Sample space for one roll: S = {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}

P(bust) = P(rolling a 1) = 16

P(safe roll) = P(not rolling a 1) = 56 — the complement of P(bust), since 16 + 56 = 1.

Every extra roll you take carries the same 16 risk — that's why "just one more roll" gets riskier the longer a turn goes on, even though the die has no memory.

Dice Cricket

You're batting. Face one ball at a time. Score runs, watch for the wicket ball, and see how your innings adds up over a full match.

  • Roll 1, 2, 3 or 4 → that many runs added to your innings.
  • Roll a 5 → OUT. Your innings ends immediately.
  • Roll a 6 → a six! 6 runs added.
  • Play 5 innings. Your match total is the sum of all innings.

The maths behind it

Sample space for one ball: S = {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}

P(out) = P(rolling a 5) = 16

P(survive the ball) = 56 — the complement of P(out).

P(hit a six) = 16, the same as P(out) — equally likely outcomes on a fair die, even though one feels much better than the other!

Compare My Two Dice
