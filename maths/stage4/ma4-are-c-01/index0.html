Scope and known limitations

This unit covers rectangles, triangles, parallelograms, circles, sectors, trapeziums, rhombuses, kites and metric area unit conversion; it assumes prior knowledge of composite-figure subdivision (Stage 3) and does not require a specific screen size, though it is best viewed on a tablet or larger.
