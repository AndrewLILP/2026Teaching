Stage 4 Mathematics / Length
MA4-LEN-C-01

Length

Applies knowledge of the perimeter of plane shapes and the circumference of circles to solve problems.

Working Mathematically (MAO-WM-01) is embedded throughout every chapter — students develop understanding and fluency by exploring and connecting mathematical concepts, choosing and applying appropriate techniques, and communicating their reasoning clearly.

How this unit works

Each chapter below teaches one sub-topic in full — a real-world Hook, explicit modelling and guided practice in Learn, independent practice in Practise, and a metacognitive Reflect close. Work through chapters in order, or jump to whichever sub-topic you need. Your progress is saved automatically on this device.

Chapters

Chapter 1
Perimeter of Quadrilaterals and Composite Figures

Solve problems involving the perimeter of parallelograms, trapeziums, rhombuses, kites and composite figures — and compare different solution methods.

~35–40 min Not started
Chapter 2
Features of a Circle

Identify and describe the radius, diameter, arc, chord, sector, segment and tangent of a circle.

~30 min Not started
Chapter 3
Circumference of a Circle

Discover that π is a constant, and apply C = πd and C = 2πr to solve circumference problems.

~35–40 min Not started
Chapter 4
Arc Length

Apply the arc length formula l = (θ/360) × 2πr to solve problems, giving exact or approximate answers.

~30–35 min Not started
Chapter 5
Perimeter of Sectors and Composite Figures

Find the perimeter of quadrants, semicircles, sectors and composite figures involving circles.

~35–40 min Not started

Scope and known limitations

This unit assumes prior fluency with perimeter of simple shapes, side properties of special quadrilaterals, comparing ratios and part-to-whole fractions, the angle sum of a revolution (360°), substitution into formulas, one-step linear equations, rounding to a specified number of decimal places, and conversion between metric units; it is designed for screens ≥360px wide and works fully offline once loaded, aside from the Google Fonts used for typography.
