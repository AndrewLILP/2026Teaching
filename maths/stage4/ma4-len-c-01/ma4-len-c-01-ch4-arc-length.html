Chapter 4

Arc Length

Which Hand Travels Further?

Look at an analogue clock.

In one hour, the tip of the minute hand sweeps all the way around the clock face — but the tip of the hour hand barely moves.

Both hands sweep for the same amount of time.

In one hour, the tip of the minute hand travels much further than the tip of the hour hand — but how much further, exactly?
I Do — Worked Examples

Watch how it’s done

A sprinkler covers a quarter-circle (90°) sector with radius 8 m.

Find the length of the curved edge (the arc).

A full circle is 360°.

This sector is 90°, which is exactly 14 of the full circle: 90 ÷ 360 = 0.25.

So the arc length is 14 of the full circumference.

l = (90360) × 2 × π × 8

l ≈ 12.6 m (to 1 decimal place)

A sector has angle 120° and radius 6 cm.
Find the arc length.

l = (θ360) × 2π·r

l = (120360) × 2 × π × 6

l ≈ 12.6 cm (to 1 decimal place)

An arc has length 15.7 cm on a circle with radius 12 cm.

Find the angle θ that the arc subtends at the centre.

Rearrange l = (θ360) × 2π·r to make θ the subject.

θ = (l ÷ 2π·r) × 360

θ = (15.7 ÷ (2 × π × 12)) × 360

θ ≈ 75.0° (to 1 decimal place)

Newman’s Word Problem

A large circular clock has a radius of 40 cm.
The minute hand moves from the 12 to the 4 in 20 minutes.
What distance does the tip of the minute hand travel during this time?
Give your answer correct to 1 decimal place.

Stage 1 — Read

Read the problem carefully.
Reread it if anything is unclear.

Stage 2 — Comprehend

What is the problem actually asking you to find?

Sentence starter: "The problem is asking me to find the distance …"

Stage 3 — Transform

The clock positions need to become an angle before you can use the arc length formula.

Sentence starter: "From 12 to 4 is … out of 12 clock positions, so the angle is … × 30° = …"

Stage 4 — Process

Carry out the arc length calculation.

Sentence starter: "l = (120360) × 2 × π × 40 = …"

Stage 5 — Encode

Write your final answer in a full sentence.

From 12 to 4 is 4 out of 12 clock positions: 4 × 30° = 120°.
l = (120360) × 2 × π × 40 ≈ 83.8 cm.
The tip of the minute hand travels approximately 83.8 cm.
