I Do — Worked Examples

Watch how it’s done

A quadrant (quarter-circle) has radius 10 cm.
Find its perimeter.

The perimeter of a sector is the two straight radii plus the curved arc.

Arc length = (90360) × 2 × π × 10 ≈ 15.7 cm

Two radii = 10 + 10 = 20 cm

Perimeter = 15.7 + 20 = 35.7 cm (to 1 decimal place)

A semicircular window has radius 4.5 m.
Find its perimeter (the frame length).

Perimeter = straight diameter edge plus semicircular arc.

Diameter = 2 × 4.5 = 9 m

Arc = (180360) × 2 × π × 4.5 ≈ 14.1 m

Perimeter = 9 + 14.1 = 23.1 m (to 1 decimal place)

A running track is a rectangle with semicircular ends.

The straight sections are each 80 m long, and the track is 60 m wide (the diameter of each semicircular end).

The two straight sections: 80 + 80 = 160 m.

The two semicircular ends together form one full circle with radius 30 m (60 m diameter ÷ 2).

Circumference = 2 × π × 30 ≈ 188.5 m

Total perimeter = 160 + 188.5 = 348.5 m (to 1 decimal place)

Newman’s Word Problem

A pizza restaurant cuts a large circular pizza (radius 15 cm) into 6 equal sectors.
A customer orders 2 adjacent sectors as one combined piece and wants to know the total edge length of foil needed to wrap just that combined piece.
This includes both straight cut edges and the curved crust edge.
Find the total perimeter of the combined piece.

Stage 5 — Encode

Write your final answer in a full sentence.

One slice is 360 ÷ 6 = 60°, so 2 slices cover 120°.
Arc = (120360) × 2 × π × 15 ≈ 31.4 cm.
Two radii = 15 + 15 = 30 cm.
Total perimeter = 31.4 + 30 = 61.4 cm.
