MA4-PRO-C-01: Probability

About this unit

This unit builds probability from the ground up: listing sample spaces and calculating theoretical probability, comparing that to what actually happens through relative frequency and simulation, understanding complementary events, and finally combining all of it to solve mixed, real-world problems.
