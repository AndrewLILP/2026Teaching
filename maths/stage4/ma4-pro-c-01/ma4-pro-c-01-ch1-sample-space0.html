I Do

Listing the sample space

The sample space is the list of every possible outcome of a chance experiment. We write it using the symbol S.

A standard die has the sample space:
S = {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}

This spinner has the sample space:
S = {R, G, W}

I Do

The probability formula

For equally likely outcomes:

P(event) = favourable outcomestotal outcomes

Worked example — rolling a 4 on a die:

There is 1 favourable outcome (rolling a 4) out of 6 total outcomes.

P(4) = 16

I Do

Every outcome, added up, equals 1

Check: the probabilities of every outcome on a die must add to 1.

16 + 16 + 16 + 16 + 16 + 16 = 1 ✓

Probabilities always sit on a scale from 0 to 1.
0 means impossible.
1 means certain.

We Do

Marbles in a bag

A bag contains 4 red, 3 blue and 2 yellow marbles — 9 marbles in total.

Together, let's find each probability:

P(red) = 49   P(blue) = 39   P(yellow) = 29

P(not yellow) = 79 (everything except the 2 yellow marbles — 4 red + 3 blue = 7).
Keep this idea in mind — we'll give it a name in Chapter 3.
