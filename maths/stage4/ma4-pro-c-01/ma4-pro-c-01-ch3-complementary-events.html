Tomorrow's Forecast

The weather forecast says there's a 70% chance of rain tomorrow.

What's the probability it doesn't rain?

Prediction locked in. Hold onto it.

I Do

What is the complement?

The complement of an event is everything in the sample space that is not the event.

A standard 6-sided die: complement of "rolling a 3" is "not rolling a 3".

S = {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}
Complement of {3} = {1, 2, 4, 5, 6}

P(3) = 16    P(not 3) = 56

Check: 16 + 56 = 1 ✓

I Do

Resolving the Hook

P(rain) = 0.7, so P(not rain) = 1 − 0.7 = 0.3.

P(not A) = 1 − P(A)

P(not rain) = 30%. This shortcut works for any event: subtract the probability from 1.

We Do

Card Draw: Not a Heart

A standard deck has 52 cards, 13 of which are hearts.

Complement of "drawing a heart" = "drawing any card that is not a heart"

P(heart) = 1352 = 14    P(not heart) = 3952 = 34
