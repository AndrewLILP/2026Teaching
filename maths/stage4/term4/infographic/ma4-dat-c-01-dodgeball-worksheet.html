?Measures of centre and spread

MeanAdd all the values, then divide by how many values there are.
MedianPut the values in order and find the middle one.
With an even number of values, find halfway between the two middle values.
ModeThe value that appears most often.
There can be more than one mode, or no mode.
RangeHighest value − lowest value. It measures spread, not centre.
OutlierA value that is far away from the rest of the data.
