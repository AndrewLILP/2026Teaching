MA4-DAT-C-01 · Data classification and visualisation · Year 8 · 60-minute lesson

Telling a story with data: statistical infographics

A teacher-led lesson using 2021 Census data about Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples.
Students revise how to match a graph to its data, then build and justify a five-panel infographic on paper.

Content point: Represent a dataset using a statistical infographic and justify the choice of graphical representation used.
Outcomes: MA4-DAT-C-01 classifies and displays data using a variety of graphical representations · MAO-WM-01 working mathematically.

Note on the data

Census counts are self-identified.
The count grows through births and because more people choose to identify.
Use “Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples”, and don’t ask Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander students to speak for their communities.
Data: Australian Bureau of Statistics (2022), Australia: Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander population summary; ABS (2022), Snapshot of Australia; ABS (2024), Understanding change in counts of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Australians.
