Learning intention: We are learning to represent parts of a whole with sector graphs, divided bar graphs and pictograms, and to compare groups of different sizes using percentages.

Graph guide: parts of a whole

The data’s job is to show…Choose…
parts of one whole (a few parts)sector graph or divided bar graph
parts of one whole, memorablypictogram (with a key, e.g. 1 icon = 12)
two or more wholes compareddivided bar graphs lined up, one under the other
counts in separate categoriescolumn or bar graph
spread of a list of numbersdot plot
Percentage = count ÷ total × 100   ·   Sector angle = percentage × 3.6  (because 360° ÷ 100 = 3.6°)
Check: percentages add to 100%, angles add to 360°.
