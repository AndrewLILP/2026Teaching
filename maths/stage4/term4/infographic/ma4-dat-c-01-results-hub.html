Win, lose, by how much? the Red Kangaroos’ season results

A teacher-led lesson using the fictional Red Kangaroos’ 8-round season.
Students calculate winning margins (including negatives), choose and justify graphs for results, a win–loss record and a ladder, then use the mean margin to find the toughest opponent.

Content points: Represent a dataset using a statistical infographic and justify the choice of graphical representation used.
Also covered: mean, median, mode and range with negative values; comparing groups using the mean (MA4-DAT-C-02).
Outcomes: MA4-DAT-C-01 classifies and displays data using a variety of graphical representations · MA4-DAT-C-02 analyses simple datasets using measures of centre, range and shape of the data · MAO-WM-01.
