Today

What we’re learning

Learning intention

We are learning to choose and justify graphs for match results, including data with negative values, and to compare groups using the mean.

Success criteria — I can…

  • calculate a winning margin, including negative margins for losses
  • choose and justify a graph for results over time, a win–loss record and a ladder
  • draw a column graph with values above and below zero
  • use the mean margin to compare how the team went against each opponent

By opponent · 5 min

Echidnas: (−3 + 2) ÷ 2 = −1 ÷ 2 = −0.5.
Wombats: (2 − 1) ÷ 2 = 0.5.

Discussion: two matches is a very small sample. The Wombats also beat the Kangaroos once. This is Panel 5 on the worksheet.

I Do · Worksheet Panel 1 · copy with me

Panel 1: a column graph above and below zero

Scale: from −4 to 5.
Zero is a bold line, not the bottom of the graph.
Wins go up from zero.
Losses go down from zero.
Add a dashed line at the mean, 1.5.
I chose a column graph because the margins are in round order and some are negative.
Bars above and below zero show wins and losses at a glance.
A sector graph would be less suitable because negative numbers can’t be slices.
