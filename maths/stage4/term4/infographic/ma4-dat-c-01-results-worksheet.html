?Working with negative margins

Adding2 + 3 + (−3) + 2 + (−1) … Start at 0 and move right for positives, left for negatives.
MeanAdd all the margins (including the negatives), then divide by how many there are.
MedianOrder from lowest to highest. Negatives come first.
With 8 values, find halfway between the 4th and 5th.
ModeThe value that appears most often.
RangeHighest − lowest. If the lowest is negative: 4 − (−3) = 4 + 3 = 7.
On the number line, count the steps from −3 to 4.
